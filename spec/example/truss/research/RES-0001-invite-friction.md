## Method

Two interviews with workspace admins in July, plus the support topic on expired invite links (SUP-0001).

## Findings

**F1.** Admins often invite people before they have an email address for them (INTV-0001 Q2).

**F2.** Contractors need access for days, not months, and admins want links to expire on their own (INTV-0002 Q1).

**F3.** Admins invite in batches on Fridays. 72-hour links expire over the weekend, before people open them (INTV-0001 Q1). Twelve tickets since June report an expired link (SUP-0001).

**F4.** Admins expect revoking an invite to take effect immediately (INTV-0002 Q2).
